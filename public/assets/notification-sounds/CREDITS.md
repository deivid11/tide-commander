# Notification tones

Source: **Kenney — Interface Sounds** (https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)** — no attribution required, kept here
out of courtesy.

The files are a curated subset of that pack, converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3 and
loudness-normalised (`loudnorm=I=-18:TP=-1.5`) so switching tones doesn't change
the perceived volume. MP3 rather than the original Ogg for Safari/iOS support.
